# Pitch examples

Fictional product: **Trackly**, a time tracker that turns tracked hours into client invoices, for solo freelancers. Sender: Jane Doe, Founder. These show shape and length. Never reuse their wording; write from the page in front of you and the sender's own voice.

## add_to_list

Notes said: list "12 Best Time Tracking Apps for Freelancers (2025)", 12 numbered entries from Toggl Track to Clockify, byline Maya Lin.

```text
Subject: Trackly for "Best Time Tracking Apps for Freelancers"

Hi Maya,

Your freelancer time tracking list has 12 apps, from Toggl Track to Clockify, and its title still says 2025.

If you refresh it for 2026, Trackly would fit in the same format:

Trackly: a time tracker that turns tracked hours into client invoices, made for freelancers who work alone.

Should I send the link and a screenshot?

Jane
```

About 55 words. With a paid line (budget above $0, a normal site):

```text
...made for freelancers who work alone.

If additions to the page are paid, we're open to that.

Should I send the link and a screenshot?
```

## German, du register

```text
Betreff: Trackly für "Die besten Zeiterfassungs-Apps für Freelancer"

Hallo Lena,

eure Liste der Zeiterfassungs-Apps für Freelancer hat 10 Einträge, von Clockify bis Timely, und im Titel steht noch 2025.

Falls ihr sie für 2026 aktualisiert, passt Trackly im gleichen Format:

Trackly: Zeiterfassung, die erfasste Stunden direkt in Rechnungen an Kunden umwandelt, gemacht für Solo-Freelancer.

Soll ich dir den Link schicken?

Jane
```

The register is du throughout, the possessive points at the company ("eure Liste") because Lena is not the byline author, and "Founder" in a signature would be "Gründerin".
