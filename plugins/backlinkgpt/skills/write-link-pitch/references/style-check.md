# Style check

Load this after a draft exists, not while writing. Naming phrases to avoid while writing makes a model reach for them. Check the draft against every item, then rewrite with the exact problems as feedback ("fix these, return the full message"). Up to three rounds.

Recipients who use AI tools daily (founders, SEOs, marketers) spot AI text about nine times in ten, mostly from vocabulary and sentence shapes, and a suspected message loses trust even when it is good. The fix is specific, true content in the sender's shape, not a humanizer pass.

## Hard fails

- Em dash or en dash anywhere, subject included (a numeric range like 3-5 is fine as a plain hyphen)
- Semicolons (Greek question marks excepted)
- Any exclamation mark in a first touch; more than one anywhere
- Placeholders: `[Name]`, `[Company]`, `{{first_name}}`, anything in brackets meant to be filled
- A link in the first touch
- Body outside 25 to 120 words for a cold email
- A subject starting with "Quick", a question-mark hook, emoji, or clickbait
- An absence claim about their page in any language: missing, lacks, skips, leaves out, doesn't include, doesn't mention, overlooks, there's no, gap (German fehlt, nicht erwähnt; French manque; Spanish falta; Dutch ontbreekt; Italian manca; Danish mangler; Polish brakuje)
- A number, claim, client or credential not in the brief's facts
- Their traffic, DR, or any SEO tool number

## Openers that read as templates

- "I hope this email finds you well", "Hope you're doing well", "Hope you are well"
- "I came across your", "I stumbled upon", "I noticed that you", "I was reading", "Saw your", "Read your", "Enjoyed your"
- "I wanted to reach out", "I'm reaching out", "Reaching out because", "Just wanted to"
- An opening sentence that summarizes their page back to them ("Your article on X covers Y", "Your guide breaks down X")
- Opening with who you are or what you built ("We built", "I'm Jane from", "I run")

## Phrases

great article, great resource, fantastic resource, valuable resource, caught my eye, love what you, big fan of, I was impressed by, resonated, highly relevant, would be a great fit, quick question, I'd love to, would you be open to, open to a quick, hop on a call, 15 minutes, value add, win-win, synergies, mutually beneficial, delve, leverage, seamless, game-changer, in today's, as a fellow, thought you might, no worries if not, looking forward to hearing from you, any thoughts?

Follow-ups: following up on my previous note, following up on my last email, I wanted to follow up, just following up, circling back, touching base, just checking in, bumping this.

LinkedIn: I came across your profile, I'd love to connect, expand my network, thought I'd connect, would be great to connect, let's connect, like-minded, pick your brain, thought leader, your journey.

AI vocabulary that keeps rising: significant, additionally, comprehensive, enhance, crucial, robust, streamline, elevate, unlock, empower, navigate, landscape, realm, tapestry, testament, pivotal, meticulous, commendable, intricate.

## Shapes

- Contrast clauses: "covers X but skips Y", "X, not Y", "isn't X, it's Y", "not only... but also", "rather than", "less X, more Y", "most X do Y, but", "while X, Y"
- "This or that" framing: "whether X or Y", "is it X, or is it Y", strings of alternatives. Pick the one that applies.
- Lists of three, stacked commands ("Pick X. Send Y. Track Z."), invented sample sizes
- Label reveals with a colon: "The fix:", "What I'd do:", "My take:"
- A tidy slogan as the last line
- Three short punchy sentences in a row; "Why? Because"
- Leaning on actually, really, exactly, super, truly; more than two "I'd"
- A generic team greeting ("Hi Team", "Hi there team", "Hi all") or a role word as a name ("Hi Redaktion")
- A stock closing question ("Would this make sense for your readers?", "Would you be open to exploring this?")
- Compliment or evaluation in the opener
- Mixed formal and informal address in one message (Sie and du, vous and tu)
- The same opener, ask or paid line as the last few messages you sent. Vary them across a batch; a whole batch with one first sentence is a template.

## Re-check

After fixing, check the whole list again on the new draft. A rewrite often fixes one tell and adds another.
